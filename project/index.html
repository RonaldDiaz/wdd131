Designed for Modern Makers & Creators

Whether you bake artisan bread, sew custom clothing, paint canvases, or develop digital templates, pricing is the foundation of a sustainable business.

CostCraft does the math for you, letting you save your calculation directly on your device so you can update prices as raw material costs change.

Direct Materials

Labor & Overhead

Your time is valuable. Calculate your hourly wage and distribute monthly fixed costs (like software, rent, and utility bills) across your production.
