CostCraft Site Plan

01

Overview

Site Name

CostCraft

This name represents the function of the site: a costing and pricing assistant for entrepreneurs.

Site Purpose

The goal of CostCraft is to help micro-entrepreneurs, artisans, crafters and creators to simplify product cost calculation. The website provides a clean and visual interface to help users calculate their direct materials, labor time and monthly overhead, and dynamically determine optimal retail prices based on their target profit margins.

Scenarios

CostCraft aims to help in scenarios like these:

Scenario A

A Craft Maker wants to know how much to charge for her new hand-crafted crochet bag design.

The user navigates to the Cost Calculator page. They enter their hourly wage and monthly overhead. They add direct material costs (like yarn, zippers, leather handles) to a list. Using a profit margin slider, they adjust the margin to see how the selling price and profit change. They bookmark the calculation using localStorage to review or modify it later.

Scenario A

A Home Baker need to update the price of his cookie recipe, because the cost of chocolate chips increased.

The user opens CostCraft on their mobile phone. The calculator retrieves their last-saved pricing calculations from localStorage, and they locate the "Chocolate Chips" material entry, update the cost, and immediately see the updated unit cost and updated suggested retail price, avoiding manual recalculations.
