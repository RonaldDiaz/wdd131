Maracaibo, Venezuela

Maracaibo is a city in the state of Zulia, in Venezuela. In "The Beloved Land of the Sun", the warmth of its people and the iconic bridge over the lake merge under a radiant, never-ending sun. Maracaibo is considered the economic center of western Venezuela due to the petroleum industry that developed along the shores of Lake Maracaibo.
